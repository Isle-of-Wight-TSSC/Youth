## North Norfolk Coastal Run

I’m thrilled to say the TSSC Youth Coastal Run was a huge success. Over 20 youth owned Triumphs joined us from all over the country.
From 1200s travelling down from Newcastle to Spitfires coming all the way from Bristol, the turnout was brilliant and the atmosphere even better.
Everyone had a fantastic time and it’s clear there’s a real enthusiasm from younger members to get involved in more TSSC events.
A brilliant success all round and proof that the passion for these cars is continuing with the next generation.
Huge thanks to the TSSC for supporting us and helping make it happen. The youth side of the club is only growing.
